# Synthetische Typen / Modelling Models
## ABK Stuttgart — Summer Semester 2026
### Prof. Daniel Koehler (Visiting Guest Professor)

## Studio Description

This studio investigates how generative AI and robotic construction
reshape architectural typology. "Synthetische Typen" (Synthetic Types)
explores the emerging relationship between computational design models
and physical construction through material supply chains, robotic
assembly, and cooperative housing models.

Students research and model new housing typologies that account for:
- AI-guided material selection and supply chain optimization
- Robotic and automated construction methods
- Cooperative ownership and community-driven development
- Sustainability and circular economy principles

## Key Themes
- **Compositional Intelligence**: How AI transforms typology from deductive to inductive composition
- **Material Supply Chains**: Understanding where building materials come from and their environmental cost
- **Robotic Assembly**: How humanoid robots, industrial arms, and automated systems change what we can build
- **Synthetic Grounds**: Architecture as manufactured ground in the AGI era

## Field Trips (South Germany)
The studio includes site visits to robotics and AI companies in the Stuttgart/Tübingen region.
