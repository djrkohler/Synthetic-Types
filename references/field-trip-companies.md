# Field Trip Companies — South Germany

## NEURA Robotics (Metzingen)
- **Focus**: Cognitive humanoid robots
- **Key Product**: MAiRA — cognitive cobot with AI perception
- **Relevance**: Next-generation robots that could work on construction sites
- **Website**: https://www.neura-robotics.com
- **Distance from Stuttgart**: ~45 min by car

## ARENA2036 (Stuttgart-Vaihingen)
- **Focus**: Active research campus — automotive, construction, digitalization
- **Key Partners**: University of Stuttgart, Bosch, Daimler, DLR
- **Relevance**: Research platform bridging automotive and construction innovation
- **Website**: https://www.arena2036.de
- **Distance from Stuttgart center**: ~20 min

## Cyber Valley (Tübingen / Stuttgart)
- **Focus**: Europe's largest AI research consortium
- **Key Institutions**: Max Planck Institute, University of Tübingen
- **Relevance**: Foundational AI research, embodied intelligence, simulation
- **Website**: https://cyber-valley.de
- **Distance from Stuttgart**: ~40 min

## Fraunhofer IPA (Stuttgart)
- **Focus**: Production technology, service robotics, cleanroom tech
- **Relevance**: Applied robotics research for manufacturing and construction
- **Website**: https://www.ipa.fraunhofer.de
- **Distance**: Central Stuttgart

## University of Stuttgart — ICD/ITKE
- **Focus**: Computational design, robotic fabrication, biomimetic construction
- **Key People**: Prof. Achim Menges (ICD), Prof. Jan Knippers (ITKE)
- **Relevance**: Pioneering robotic construction in architecture
- **Website**: https://www.icd.uni-stuttgart.de

## Holcim Innovation Center (Holderbank, CH — optional)
- **Focus**: Cement, concrete, carbon capture, 3D printing
- **Relevance**: Largest building materials company, sustainability R&D
- **Website**: https://www.holcim.com
- **Note**: Located in Switzerland, ~3h from Stuttgart
