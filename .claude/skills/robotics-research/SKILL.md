# Robotics Research Skill

When the student asks about a robotics topic, company, or application:

## Step 1: Technology Overview
- What type of robot/system? (industrial arm, mobile robot, humanoid, drone, swarm)
- Key technical specifications (payload, reach, precision, speed)
- Sensors and perception capabilities
- Software and control systems
- AI and machine learning integration

## Step 2: Manufacturer/Research Lab Profile
- Company or institution background
- Key products or research projects
- Funding, scale, partnerships
- For field trip companies (NEURA, KUKA, Fraunhofer IPA, etc.), research deeply
- Search for recent news, press releases, product launches

## Step 3: Construction Applications
- Current uses in construction (if any)
- Research prototypes and experimental projects
- Which construction tasks can this robot perform?
- What are the integration challenges? (site conditions, coordination, regulations)
- Compare with manual construction: speed, cost, quality, safety

## Step 4: Architecture Research Context
- Relevant academic research (ICD/ITKE Stuttgart, ETH Zurich, MIT, etc.)
- ACADIA, Rob|Arch, or other conference papers
- Simulation environments used (Isaac Sim, Genesis, etc.)
- Connection to the studio's "Synthetische Typen" theme

## Step 5: Video Research
- Search YouTube for demo videos, conference presentations, construction footage
- Use YouTube transcript MCP to analyze relevant videos
- Note key timestamps and claims

## Step 6: Write and Save
- Follow the document template from CLAUDE.md
- Save to `research/robotics-[topic-slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`
