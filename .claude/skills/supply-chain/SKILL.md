# Supply Chain Research Skill

When the student names a material or construction component:

## Step 1: Map the Supply Chain
Research and document each stage:
1. **Raw Material Extraction** — Where does it come from? Mining, harvesting, quarrying?
2. **Processing** — How is raw material refined? Chemical processes, mechanical processing?
3. **Manufacturing** — How is the building product made? What factories, what processes?
4. **Transport & Logistics** — How does it get to the construction site? Distances, modes, carbon cost?
5. **On-Site Assembly** — How is it installed? Labor, equipment, skill requirements?
6. **End of Life** — Can it be recycled, reused, composted, or must it go to landfill?

## Step 2: Environmental Analysis
- Embodied carbon (kgCO₂e per unit)
- Energy intensity of production
- Water usage
- Waste generation
- Comparison with alternative materials
- Circular economy potential

## Step 3: Industry Landscape
- Key manufacturers (global and European/German where possible)
- Market size and trends
- Recent innovations or disruptions
- Relevant standards and certifications (DIN, EN, ISO)

## Step 4: Architectural Relevance
- Which building types commonly use this material?
- What are the structural, thermal, acoustic properties?
- What are the design constraints and opportunities?
- Notable architectural projects using this material

## Step 5: Write and Save
- Follow the document template from CLAUDE.md
- Include a supply chain diagram description (text-based flow)
- Save to `research/supply-chain-[material-slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`
