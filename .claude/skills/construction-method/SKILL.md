# Construction Method Research Skill

When the student names a construction method or technique:

## Step 1: Technical Description
- What is the method? Clear definition and classification
- Step-by-step construction process
- Tools, equipment, and machinery required
- Material requirements and specifications
- Typical tolerances and quality standards

## Step 2: Labor & Skills
- What skills are required? Specialized trades?
- Training requirements
- Team size and composition
- Safety considerations
- Potential for automation or robotic assistance

## Step 3: Performance Analysis
- Speed of construction (m²/day, units/week, etc.)
- Cost comparison with conventional methods
- Structural performance
- Thermal and acoustic performance
- Durability and maintenance requirements

## Step 4: Advantages & Limitations
- What does this method do better than alternatives?
- What are the constraints and challenges?
- Climate and geographic limitations
- Scale limitations (single house vs. high-rise?)
- Regulatory status (building codes, approvals)

## Step 5: Case Studies
- Search for 3-5 notable built projects using this method
- Include project name, architect, location, year, scale
- What worked well? What were the challenges?
- Search YouTube for construction documentation videos

## Step 6: Write and Save
- Follow the document template from CLAUDE.md
- Save to `research/construction-[method-slug]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`
